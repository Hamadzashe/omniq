Capabilities

Where complexity meets clear ownership.

From strategy to live operations, OMNIQ covers the full stack of integrated systems work so you are not left managing a patchwork of vendors.

  1. 01

    Systems management & integration

    We connect your existing software, hardware, and business processes so information flows cleanly and teams work from one reliable picture of operations.

  2. 02

    IT consultancy & software development

    We advise on technology choices and build custom software that matches how your organisation works, from internal tools to customer facing platforms.

  3. 03

    Network infrastructure & databases

    We design, manage, and maintain networks and databases so your systems stay available, organised, and ready to scale as demand grows.

  4. 04

    Cybersecurity

    We put practical security controls in place and keep them current so your systems, accounts, and sensitive information stay protected against everyday and advanced threats.

  5. 05

    Data Protection Officer (DPO) services

    We provide Data Protection Officer support to help your organisation handle personal data lawfully, document privacy practices, respond to data subject requests, and stay aligned with data protection requirements.

  6. 06

    Cloud computing & automation

    We move workloads to the cloud and automate repeatable tasks so teams spend less time on manual work and more time on delivery.

  7. 07

    Hardware supply, installation & support

    We supply, install, maintain, and support computers, servers, and related technology so your physical equipment stays reliable from day one.
